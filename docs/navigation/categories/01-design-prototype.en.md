# Game design & prototyping

[中文](01-design-prototype.md) · [All categories](../README.en.md)

## When to use

Session goals, rules or the core experience are unclear, or a design hypothesis needs comparison.

## Inputs and output

- Inputs: Players/devices, idea, established rules, time and authorized scope.
- Output: A compact prototype plan: input, state, ending conditions, feedback, counterexample and open decisions.

## Starting routes

- **New project:** Start with one reversible hypothesis; do not invent unspecified gameplay values.
- **Existing project:** Reproduce the current loop and separate the experiment from established rules.

## Available entry

[game-prototype-planning](../../../skills/game-prototype-planning/SKILL.md). Read the actual entry and only its needed local references.

## Smallest check

Choose one hypothesis and a counterexample; define an observation that would reject it. A finished plan is not a successful playtest.
