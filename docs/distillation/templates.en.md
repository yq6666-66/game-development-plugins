# Research, candidate and weekly templates

[中文](templates.md) · [Tutorial](README.en.md)

Copy only relevant fields. These are templates, not completed research records.

## Daily: `docs/research/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`

- Date, repository, actual default branch, baseline commit and working branch.
- Prior records/unmerged branches checked; topic count and reasons for fewer topics.
- Per topic: original URL, author, date, version/commit and inspected portions.
- Problem/audience, mechanism, steps/tradeoffs, conditions and inputs/outputs.
- Minimal verification, failure modes, costs and exclusions.
- Source status: body/transcript/code inspected, author-only claim, or inaccessible lead.
- Execution status: environment/results or not run; separate your own inference.
- Related Skill/gap, proposed files and improvement criteria.
- New value from repeated sources; actual changes, checks, commit links and blockers.

## Candidate: `docs/research/candidates/YYYY-MM-DD/topic.md`

- Claim and sources; distinguish source conclusions from your synthesis.
- Trigger, conditions, inputs/outputs and minimal workflow.
- Why it fits an existing Skill or warrants a distinct Skill.
- Proposed files, compatibility risks, counterexample, minimal test and acceptance.
- Status: lead / draft / unverified / reviewable / deferred; evidence and next step.

## Weekly: `docs/research/weekly/YYYY-Www.md`

- Previous calendar-week interval in Asia/Shanghai, covered dates and gaps.
- Current default-branch baseline; included daily branches/SHAs; merged/deduplicated work.
- Valuable findings, shared methods, differences/conflicts and integrated files.
- Priority, costs, verification, exclusions/reasons and next week's questions.
- Per synthesis judgment: statement, evidence, reasoning, scope, uncertainty, counterexample, minimal test and plugin impact.
- Separate package checks, scenario evaluation, host discovery and game execution; branch/commit links.
